# Changelog

## Beta 1

Initial beta distribution of RealtekVTDNext for the chips in the [compatibility table](docs/HARDWARE.md).

Includes AppleVTD and NoIOMMU DMA paths, IPv4/IPv6 checksum handling, TSO with chip-specific fallbacks, negotiated PAUSE, software VLAN and resource-guarded Jumbo. The accepted executable is unchanged; this update simplifies names, packaging and documentation.
